Kitchen Hot Water

Food-Service Hot Water Systems Built for the Rush

A commercial kitchen is one of the hardest places to keep supplied with hot water. Prep sinks, pre-rinse sprayers, and dish cycles all draw from the same tank, a lunch rush can outrun a small heater quickly, and a high-temperature dish machine depends on a booster to reach its final rinse temperature. Rio Rancho Water Heater sizes, repairs, and maintains the tanks, tankless units, and booster heaters that keep the kitchen and restrooms supplied, and we plan service around your hours.

Many restaurant inspection problems trace back to water temperature. If the main heater falls behind, handwashing sinks run lukewarm, the three-compartment sink can't hold its wash temperature, and the dish machine struggles to sanitize at peak service. We verify temperatures at the tank, the booster, and the fixtures and document every visit in written reports your inspector and landlord can review.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Our technicians are used to working in occupied facilities, installing during off-hours when needed, minimizing disruption, and staging temporary hot water whenever the layout allows.

Code & Inspections

Safe Installations, Documented Service

A poorly installed or neglected water heater can create carbon monoxide hazards, scalding, bacterial growth in lukewarm storage, and costly water damage from a ruptured tank. That's a real liability for a commercial property owner with employees, customers, and tenants in the building. Our commercial installations and inspections cover venting, combustion air, gas line sizing, relief valve discharge, expansion tanks, mixing valves, and code items like drain pans and seismic straps where required, followed by a written report that ranks each risk by priority and recommends specific fixes.

Regular professional service protects your people and your property, and acting on expert recommendations keeps small issues from becoming emergencies. It also builds a paper trail. We keep service logs, temperature records, and a binder of reports for every account, with more frequent visits for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and central plant rooms. That record shows landlords, insurers, and inspectors that you've done your due diligence.
