Warning Signs to Watch For

Catching a symptom early is often the difference between a quick part swap and a tank that gives out on the garage floor. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Higher gas or electric bills for less hot water
  • A water heater that is more than about ten years old
  • Discolored hot water, a sign the tank is corroding inside
  • A slow leak or damp spot under the tank itself
  • Sediment noise that keeps getting louder
  • Hot water running out faster every month
  • Rust or corrosion on the tank, fittings, or vent connector
  • Repairs that keep coming back, one part after another

How to Prevent Water Heater Problems

A few preventive steps can help you avoid a cold shower or a wet floor down the road. Follow these tips from our technicians:

  • Set the thermostat around 120 degrees to prevent scalding and reduce wear
  • Have the anode rod checked around year three to five and replaced if it is worn
  • Clean the air filter on a heat pump water heater every few months
  • Tankless units need a yearly descaling, especially where the water is hard
  • Plan ahead: knowing the heater's age lets you replace it on your schedule, not after a leak
  • Flush the new tank once or twice a year to keep sediment from building up
  • Keep household water pressure in a safe range to protect the tank and its fittings
